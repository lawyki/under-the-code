**The method for Gate 2 repairs, proved on Fig 1.1 (2026-10-12): anchor, map, run, teach back.** (1) One-sentence message a newcomer could repeat. (2) Anchor: a real thing the reader already knows that does the same job, drawn as part 1. (3) Map: the computer version as part 2, each part named by its counterpart. (4) Run: one concrete example, step by step. (5) Teach back: a fresh newcomer reader sees only the picture (bare.mjs frames) and must retell it and say what stopped them; their words name the fix, one revision, retest. On Fig 1.1 three blind readers scored the old binary version and the tally version "partly" and the anchored version "yes" (UNDER.md §4ac). No figure reaches the owner without passing the teach-back.
