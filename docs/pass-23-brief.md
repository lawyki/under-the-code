## The brief

Read the entire book in order — cover, Parts I–V, the Bridge in place,
every part opener and closer, the glossary page last — as a committed
student reading it the way the owner is about to. Judge only what per-part
passes structurally could not see:

1. **Accumulation.** Does each layer genuinely stand on the previous one?
   When a late chapter leans on an early concept (two's complement, the
   MMU walk, quorum arithmetic), does the earlier treatment carry the
   weight at the distance it's used from — or has the reader lost it by
   then?
2. **The seams at book altitude.** Each closer→opener seam was verified
   locally; read them as a sequence. Does the handoff rhythm hold across
   four part boundaries, or does it become formula?
3. **Promise accounting.** Everything the front of the book promises
   (cover, Part I opener, chapter leads that point forward) — is every
   promise cashed, and cashed where it was promised?
4. **The recurring instruments.** Five closer stamps, the "what you now
   understand" codas, the insight strips, the Bridge as calibration
   standard — do they accumulate power through variation, or wear through
   repetition, when met in one continuous read?
5. **The altitude profile.** Where does the book's difficulty spike or
   sag across the full arc? Is the hardest stretch (likely Ch14 math or
   Ch16 concurrency) survivable with only what the book itself has
   taught?
6. **The ending.** Does Ch18 earn the synthesis — does the trace land as
   the payoff of seventeen chapters, or as a recap?

**Method:** your own continuous deep read is the instrument. Independent
agents only where they add a lens you can't hold (e.g. one fresh reader
who starts at Part III to test whether mid-book entry is survivable —
a real reader behaviour the linear read can't simulate). No adversarial
defense theatre — this is one reader's considered report, ranked.

**Register:** findings are ranked by how much they'd matter to a real
student, each with location, symptom, and a one-line proposed direction —
never a rewrite. "The arc holds, here are the three places it strains" is
a fully successful outcome. Manufacturing findings to justify the pass is
the failure mode; the four prior verifications' blind readers already
rated every part at bar individually.
