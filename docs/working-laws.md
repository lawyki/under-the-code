# Working laws (how the agent works on this book)

Written 2026-10-12 after the owner rejected Pass 29 Part I ("the part one was a failure, but you cannot also use 23 million tokens"). Binding on every session, next to the four laws in CLAUDE.md.

1. **The result is the only measure.** A passage or figure is done when a reader understands it at the right depth (law 4). Gates (dashes, layout, tests, budget) are hygiene: passing them never makes anything done, and a report never says "done" on the strength of a gate.
2. **I write; agents test.** One author edits the prose, reading each section whole, so the book keeps one voice. No committee chains (no cold reader, teacher, advocate, gate loops). Agents only read blind and report what stopped them.
3. **I read every changed sentence as the owner will,** before it ships. The owner reads every word; "merely correct" is not done.
4. **Samples before scale.** Three edited paragraphs go to the owner before a pass touches a chapter. Rejected samples stop the pass.
5. **Small batches.** One chapter per commit. A wrong direction may cost one chapter, never a part.
6. **A fixed budget, reported.** The text pass for the whole book stays under 5 million tokens: about 40 thousand per section on average. Per chapter: my own edit, one blind newcomer reader and one expert checker (Sonnet 5.5) over the chapter's changed paragraphs, one revision. The tokens each chapter used go into the ledger.
7. **Simpler, never shallower; deeper only where it carries the job** (law 4). A gap is closed by making the passage clearer, never by adding expert detail, and never by replacing a mechanism with a slogan.
8. **Facts stay true.** §4g is law; a new fact is added only when the passage needs it, and only with a source opened.
9. **Report honestly and briefly:** what changed, what was checked, what was not.
