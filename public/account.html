What this stores — a privacy notice.

The book itself collects nothing: no analytics, no trackers, no third-party requests. Reading progress for signed-out readers lives in your browser's localStorage and never leaves your device. An account adds exactly one thing — the same reading position, stored server-side so it can follow you between devices. In detail:

  • What is stored: your email address; a session identifier (only its hash is kept); and one reading position — which part of the book, a paragraph anchor, an offset within it, chapter and section titles, and when it was last updated. That is the whole database.
  • What it is for: signing you in and putting you back at your paragraph. Nothing else. Your address receives only the sign-in links you yourself request — no announcements, no marketing — and is never shared or sold.
  • Where it lives: a Cloudflare D1 database in the European Union, the same infrastructure that already serves the book. Sign-in mail is delivered by Cloudflare Email Sending — our host's own service, no third-party mail provider.
  • Retention: sign-in links expire after 15 minutes and work once; sessions expire after 180 days; the reading position is overwritten in place as you read and exists only while your account does.
  • Deletion: the button above removes your email, sessions, and position immediately and completely — or write to hello@atheric.eu.
