Your marks

    What this stores — a privacy notice.

    The book itself collects nothing: no analytics, no trackers, no third-party requests. Reading progress for signed-out readers lives in your browser's localStorage and never leaves your device. An account adds exactly one thing — the same reading position, stored server-side so it can follow you between devices. In detail:

    • What is stored: your email address and when you confirmed it; if you set a password, a salted, peppered PBKDF2 hash of it (never the password itself); for each passkey, its public key, a label, and when it was added and last used (the private key never leaves your device); a session identifier (only its hash is kept); and one reading position — which part of the book, a paragraph anchor, an offset within it, chapter and section titles, and when it was last updated. Briefly, the database also holds hashed one-time sign-in links and passkey challenges until they are used or expire, and — to slow down password guessing and mail abuse — counters keyed by a keyed hash of the address or network, never the address itself, cleared after about a day. Nothing else.
    • What it is for: signing you in and putting you back at your paragraph. Nothing else. Your address receives only the links you yourself request and a short notice whenever your sign-in settings change — no announcements, no marketing — and is never shared or sold.
    • Where it lives: a Cloudflare D1 database in the European Union, the same infrastructure that already serves the book. Mail is delivered by Cloudflare Email Sending — our host's own service, no third-party mail provider.
    • Retention: emailed links work once — confirmation links expire after 24 hours, sign-in links after 30 minutes; an account whose email is never confirmed is removed after 14 days; sessions expire after 180 days; the reading position is overwritten in place as you read and exists only while your account does. Expired records are swept automatically every few hours.
    • Deletion: the button above removes your email, password, passkeys, sessions and position immediately and completely — or write to hello@atheric.eu.
